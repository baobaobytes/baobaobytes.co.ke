Our promises

Six things we always do

👥

Two adults present

No adult is ever alone with a child, in person or online.

✍️

Parent consent first

Parents or carers agree before any session, photo or follow-up.

🔒

Minimum data

We collect only what a session needs, keep it securely and never sell or share it.

📷

Photos only with permission

No child’s photo, name or school is published without written consent.

📵

No private contact

Facilitators don’t message children privately or connect with them on social media.

🧭

Clear referral path

If a child shares something worrying, we follow a set referral process, with Childline and the authorities where needed.

This website

We don’t collect your data here

The games and tools on baobaobytes.co.ke run entirely on your own device. We don’t ask for names, we don’t use tracking cookies and there are no sign-up forms.

The site remembers only one thing: whether you turned on low-data mode. That setting stays in your own browser.

Our fonts load from Google Fonts. To avoid that, use low-data mode, or ask us for an offline version for schools.

Sessions and programmes

How we run sessions

  • Facilitators complete safeguarding training and background checks before working with children.
  • Parents or carers receive a letter explaining the session before it happens.
  • Children are grouped by age path, so content always fits their age.
  • Any information we collect is handled in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.
  • Partners who host sessions agree to these standards in writing.

Report a concern

Worried about a child, or about us?

If you are worried about how a BaobaoBytes facilitator, volunteer or partner has behaved, tell our safeguarding lead. Every report is taken seriously and handled confidentially.
