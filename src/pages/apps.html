Who Knows: Kenya

by OHB Studio · developer Bradley Omondi · Android

Rated 3+Geography quizPlays offline2 players on one phone

A quiz game about Kenya’s 47 counties and 290 constituencies. Work your way from 001 Mombasa to 047 Nairobi, then take on harder rounds. Learn mode has facts and maps for every county.

7 ways to play
  1. Number to county
  2. County to number
  3. Name it from its shape
  4. Which county are these constituencies in?
  5. Tap the county on the map
  6. Word search: counties
  7. Word search: constituencies
Why we like it

It’s made in Kenya, about Kenya, and turns social studies into a game. Two players can share one phone, or friends can play together over the same Wi-Fi. That makes it great for family game nights, classrooms and revision.

B³ verdict: great to play together

Excellent Kenyan learning content that works offline. Before handing it to a child, turn on purchase approval in Google Play (Settings → Authentication → Require authentication for purchases). The app also shares device IDs and approximate location with other companies, so younger children should play with a grown-up.

The B³ safe app check

Six questions every app must pass

Parents can use the same questions for any app. On Google Play, scroll to “Data safety” and the age rating before you install.

🎂

Right age rating

The store rating fits the age of the child who will use it.

💬

No strangers

No open chat with strangers, or chat that is moderated and can be switched off.

📺

Fair ads

No ads, or only child-appropriate ads that don’t trick kids into tapping.

💳

No pushy purchases

No pressure to buy, and purchases can be locked behind a parent’s approval.

🔒

Minimum data

Collects only what it needs, with no location tracking or selling of data.

🌍

Worth the screen time

It teaches, creates or connects, ideally with local content kids recognise.
