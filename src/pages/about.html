About BaobaoBytes · B³

Growing safe, smart young creators

Kenyan children are online earlier than ever: gaming, creating, learning and earning. BaobaoBytes helps them do it with confidence, and helps the adults around them keep up.

🌳

Why a baobab?

Deep roots, wide shade

The baobab grows for centuries. It stores water for the dry season and offers shade where communities gather to talk, learn and decide things together.

That’s our model. The roots are parents, teachers and carers. The trunk is the safety knowledge every child needs. The branches are three age paths that grow with each child. The bytes are the small, practical lessons that keep them safe online.

Chipukizi“Sprout”. Ages 9–11, learning through play.
Wabunifu“Creators”. Ages 12–14, learning through making.
Walinzi“Guardians”. Ages 15–18, learning through defending.
Wazazi“Parents”. The roots that hold it all up.

Our approach

How we teach

🎯

Right for their age

A 9-year-old and a 17-year-old face different risks. Each path is built for its age group.

🎮

Learn by doing

Games, simulations and real Kenyan examples instead of lectures and fear.

📣

Children’s voice

Children help design what we teach, and older learners teach younger ones.

📶

Works for everyone

Low-data mode, English and Kiswahili, and sessions that work without devices.

Who we are

Led by Charity Muigai

BaobaoBytes is led by Charity Muigai, working with facilitators, educators and young people across Kenya to make online safety practical, local and fun.

We work alongside schools, child-focused organisations and creative communities that share our belief that children should be safe online and also free to create, learn and lead there.

Our mission

Every Kenyan child explores the internet with confidence, and knows exactly who to turn to when something goes wrong.
