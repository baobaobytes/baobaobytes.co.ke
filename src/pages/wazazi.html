Six habits

What matters more than any app

💬

Talk early, talk often

Ask what they enjoy online before asking what went wrong. Small daily chats make the big ones easier.

😌

Stay calm when they tell you

Thank them for telling you. Taking the phone away teaches them to hide things next time.

🔐

Set up together

Turn on privacy settings, screen locks and two-step login side by side, so they understand why.

📸

Save, block, report

If someone is harmful: screenshot the evidence, block them, report on the app, and get help.

🗝️

Codes are never shared

The family rule: nobody real ever needs your PIN, password or SMS code. Not even “M-PESA”.

🌙

Phones sleep outside bedrooms

A shared charging spot at night protects sleep and cuts late-night pressure from chats.

Our family online agreement

    If something has happened

    Four steps, in this order

    1. Stay calm, thank them

      “Thank you for telling me. You’re not in trouble.” This keeps them talking.

    2. Save the evidence

      Screenshot messages, usernames and links before anything is deleted.

    3. Block and report

      Block the account and report it on the app. Don’t reply, and never pay a blackmailer.

    4. Get support

      Call Childline on 116 for free advice. If your child is in danger, call 999 or 112.
