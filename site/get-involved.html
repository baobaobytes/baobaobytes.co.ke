A typical school session

From first message to badges

  1. Plan together

    We agree the age groups, numbers, date and space with your school.

  2. Parents informed

    A consent letter goes home, in English and Kiswahili, before the session.

  3. Session day

    Two facilitators run age-path games and discussions. They work with or without devices.

  4. Follow-up

    Children take home badges and a family agreement. Schools get a short summary.
