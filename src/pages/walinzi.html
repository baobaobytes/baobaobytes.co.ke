Account lock-down meter

Open your main account’s settings and tick each one as you do it. Nothing is saved or sent.

Exposed · 0%

Rule zero

Never share a one-time code, PIN or password. No real company, friend or “M-PESA agent” needs it.

If you shared one by accident: change your password now, log out other devices, and tell someone you trust.

Scam radar

Six red flags

One flag means slow down. Two or more means it’s almost certainly a scam.

⏱️ Hurry!

“Only 10 minutes left.” Pressure stops you thinking.

💸 Pay first

Registration fees, deposits, “unlock” payments.

🔑 Send the code

Anyone asking for an OTP, PIN or password.

🎁 Too good

KSh 3,000 a day for liking videos. Free phones. Big prizes.

🤫 Go private

“Let’s move to Telegram or WhatsApp.” Away from reporting tools.

😨 Threats

“Pay or I’ll share it.” Don’t pay. Get help.
