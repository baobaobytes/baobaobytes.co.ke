How it works

Learn it, play it, share it

  1. Pick your path

    Choose the branch for your age. Each one has its own look, games and challenges.

  2. Play and practise

    Try real situations, like a stranger in a game chat or a fake M-PESA message, with no risk.

  3. Earn your badges

    Safe Online, Creator, Defender and more. Each badge shows a skill you’ve practised.

  4. Teach someone

    Show a friend, sibling or parent one trick you spotted. That’s how the tree grows.
